How it works

Scan. Review. Protect. Share.

From the photo you’re proud of to a copy that’s safe to post — all on your phone.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Choose a photo, share one from another app or take one with the SpotShield camera. Six checks run on the phone.

  2. 02

    Review

    See everything that could reveal the place, from location data in the file to a sign far in the background.

  3. 03

    Protect

    Cover, pixelate, blur or fill each area — or protect every risky one in a single tap. Fill rebuilds the area from its surroundings.

  4. 04

    Share

    Get a new copy without location data, checked once more before you share. The photo in your gallery stays as it was.

What SpotShield looks for

  • Location data in the file
  • Signs and text
  • Number plates
  • Map and sonar screens
  • QR codes and barcodes
  • Buildings and skylines
  • Hidden video in motion photos

Privacy

Your photos never leave your device.

Every app checks and protects your photos on your phone, even offline. The apps don’t have internet permission, so nothing can be uploaded — not to us, not to anyone.

  • No internet permission

    Android keeps the apps offline, so nothing can be uploaded.

  • No account

    No sign-up, no email, no profile.

  • No analytics, no ads

    The only permissions are the camera and, for “Check original”, the photo’s own location tag — both optional.

Honest limits

What SpotShield can’t see

Some clues are beyond any app. Check these yourself before you share.

  • Whether the place itself is recognisable, even without buildings or a skyline

  • Distinctive vegetation and shorelines

  • Sun position and shadows

  • Reflections in water, glasses or windows

  • What other people post from the same trip

  • When you post
